1. FooDD (Food Detection Dataset)Introduced by: Parisa Pouladzadeh, Abdulsalam Yassine, and Shervin Shirmohammadi (University of Ottawa).Setup & Details:Contains approximately 3,000 food images spanning single items and mixed food plates captured under varying lighting, angles, and phone cameras.  Crucially, every plate image includes a human thumb positioned beside the plate as the physical calibration marker.
Access Links:Kaggle Mirror: FooDD IEEE Datasets on Kaggle (Readily downloadable structured archive).Dataset Paper & Release Info: ResearchGate: FooDD Publication.
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/darsh22blc1378/foodd-ieee-datasets


2. ECUSTFD (ECUST Food Dataset)Introduced by: Yanchao Liang and Jianmin Li (East China University of Science and Technology).Setup & Details:Specifically designed for volume-based food portion estimation using two views: Top View (~0°) and Side View (~90°).  Contains 2,978 multi-view images covering 19 food types on plates.  Includes ground-truth volume (measured via water displacement), electronic scale weight/mass records, food density values (density.xls), and bounding box annotations.  Uses a standardized One-Yuan coin (diameter: 25.0 mm) as the calibration reference.  
https://github.com/Liang-yc/ECUSTFD-resized-/blob/master/JPEGImages/apple001S(1).JPG

3. Food-101 (Used for Classification Baselines)
Introduced by: Lukas Bossard, Matthieu Guillaumin, and Luc Van Gool (ETH Zurich).
Setup & Details: Frequently paired with the datasets above to pre-train deep neural network backbones (CNNs/ViTs) for multi-class food recognition before volume modeling.
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/dansbecker/food-101
